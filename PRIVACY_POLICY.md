# Privacy Policy

## News Video Generator

This application (News Video Generator) collects and processes publicly available news and media content.

We do not collect personal user data such as names, emails, or passwords.

The application uses third-party APIs such as NewsAPI, Pexels, and Wikipedia to fetch content.

No personal data is stored, shared, or sold.

Generated content (audio and video) is used only for content creation and is not permanently stored.

We respect all third-party API policies and content usage guidelines.
